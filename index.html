Clink Different

2023 - Present

Collaborated with RFBinder to create a website that promotes Wines of Germany and Bordeaux Wines throughout the United States.

You might think these two regions are different, but look more closely, and you’ll discover fascinating parallels. They share history, amazing landscapes, culture, culinary passions and a modern outlook on the wine traditions of old. There is more to these renowned regions than meets the eye: Stunning red wines from Germany. Delicious white wines from Bordeaux. Explore surprising styles, discover unparalleled wine-sipping destinations, and dive into our culinary culture. Challenge your perceptions (and your palates).
